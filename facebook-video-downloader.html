How to Use the Facebook Video Tool

  1. Open a Facebook video you have permission to save.
  2. Copy the video's URL.
  3. Paste the link into the input box above.
  4. Press Check Video Link to validate the URL format.
  5. Use a compatible download service when one is connected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I access every Facebook video?

No. Availability depends on the video's privacy settings, whether it still exists and the capabilities of the connected service.

Does checking a link download the video?

No. This page checks the URL format only. Actual video processing requires a connected and functioning backend.

Is SocialToolHub an official Facebook service?

No. SocialToolHub is an independent website and is not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.

Why might a video link fail?

The link may be incorrect, the video may have been removed, or the video may not be publicly accessible.
