Frequently Asked Questions

What is SocialToolHub?

SocialToolHub is an online media utility that provides a media downloader and a video-to-script transcription tool.

Can I download any private video?

No. The service is intended for publicly accessible content that you are legally allowed to access, download or transcribe.

Does the transcript translate the video?

The transcription system attempts to detect the spoken language and generates text in that spoken language rather than intentionally translating it into another language.

About SocialToolHub
